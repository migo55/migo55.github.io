a working document — edit it as things firm up. It assumes Corner Lot Studio is a
**creative studio with a physical location**, so the SEO strategy leans heavily
on **local search** — getting found by people nearby who are looking for what you
offer.

## 4. Social channels

For a visual/creative studio, **show the work**. Pick one primary channel and go
deep; the studio's output is your best marketing.

| Channel | Best for | Priority |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Instagram** | Showing the space + finished work; local discovery via location tags & hashtags | High for most studios |
| **Google Business Profile posts** | Local reach, reviews, updates | High |
| **Facebook** | Local community, events, older demographics | Medium |
| **TikTok / Reels** | Behind-the-scenes, reach, personality | Medium–High |
| **Pinterest** | If output is highly visual/aspirational | Situational |

> Primary channel: ____________________

**Reviews are marketing.** After every happy client, ask for a Google review.
Volume + recency of reviews is one of the strongest local ranking signals, and
it's the first thing new customers look at.

## 6. Cadence (keep it sustainable)

- **2–3 posts/week** on your primary channel to start. Studios have visual
  output ready-made — use it.
- **Batch it**: one shoot/project → multiple posts (the work, the process, the
  result, the review).
- **Always tag your location** and use a small set of local + specialty hashtags.

---

## 7. Measuring what works

Check monthly:

- **Google Business Profile insights** — searches, calls, direction requests,
  website clicks. For a local studio this is your most important dashboard.
- **Google Search Console** — which search terms bring people to the site.
- **Instagram/channel insights** — which posts drove profile visits and inquiries.
- **Your north-star number** (inquiries/bookings) — track this one closely.
